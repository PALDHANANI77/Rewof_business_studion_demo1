GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Not sure where to start? Here are a few things founders and business owners often ask.

How long does a website take? +

Small business sites often take 5–10 working days after the scope and content are clear. Larger or more custom projects take longer.

Do I need to provide photos and text? +

Business details, photos, product names, and service information help us build the site accurately. We can also guide content structure if needed.

Is domain and hosting included? +

Not by default. We explain domain, hosting, and recurring costs before work begins so the quote is fair and transparent.

Can customers contact me through WhatsApp? +

Yes. We can add WhatsApp call-to-action buttons with prefilled messages for quick, direct enquiries.

Will my website work on mobile? +

Yes. Every site is designed mobile-first and tested across common phone, tablet, and desktop layouts.
